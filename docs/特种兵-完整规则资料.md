THROAT SLITTAS
Unusual amongst Orks, Kommandos utilise stealth and
misdirection to close in before falling upon the enemy
with jagged blades drawn.
Each friendly KOMMANDO operative (excluding
BOMB SQUIG) can perform the Charge action
while it has a Conceal order.
