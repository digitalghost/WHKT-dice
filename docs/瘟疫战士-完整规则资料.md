**中文全文**

这些经受了基因强化的超人们的存在意义只
有一个：战争。
在每一名己方瘟疫战士特工的激活期间，特工
可以执行两次近战行动或者两次射击行动。如果
选择执行两次射击行动，那么其中至少有一次必
须选择使用一件爆矢手枪、爆矢枪或灵能武器进
行攻击。您在每一次激活中不能多次选择同一件
灵能远程武器。
每一名己方瘟疫战士特工可以进行反应，无论
其拥有的命令。

**English full text**

PLAGUE MARINE
FACTION RULE
ASTARTES
Space Marines are genetically augmented transhumans
forged for only one purpose: war.
During each friendly PLAGUE MARINE
operative’s activation, it can perform either two
Shoot actions or two Fight actions. If it’s two
Shoot actions, a bolt pistol, boltgun or PSYCHIC
weapon must be selected for at least one of them.
You cannot select the same PSYCHIC ranged
weapon more than once per activation.
Each friendly PLAGUE MARINE operative can
counteract regardless of its order.

### 2. 毒素（POISON）

**卡图**：[11-02-阵营规则-毒素.png](瘟疫战士-卡片提取/11-02-阵营规则-毒素.png)

**源页**：PDF 第 3 页

**中文全文**

纳垢能够制造各种被亚空间腐蚀的剧毒、毒
液、病毒和灵魂上的疾病，能够超越一切自
然的抵抗力和医疗措施。
本杀戮小队中特工装备的一些武器拥有“毒素”
武器规则。
*毒素：在结算成功骰子步骤中，如果您使用任
意成功造成了任何伤害，那么成为该武器攻击目
标的特工（己方瘟疫战士特工除外）在流程结
束时获得一枚己方毒素指示物（如果特工还没有
获得毒素指示物)。每当一名拥有己方毒素指示
物的特工被激活时，对其造成 1 点伤害。

**English full text**

PLAGUE MARINE
FACTION RULE
POISON
Nurgle deals in warp-tainted poisons, toxins, viral
plagues and sicknesses of the soul that defy both
natural resilience and medical intervention.
Some weapons in this team’s rules have the
Poison weapon rule.
*Poison: In the Resolve Attack Dice step, if you
inflict damage with any successes, the operative
this weapon is being used against (excluding
friendly PLAGUE MARINE operatives) gains one
of your Poison tokens (if it doesn’t already have
one). Whenever an operative that has one of your
Poison tokens is activated, inflict 1 damage on it.
