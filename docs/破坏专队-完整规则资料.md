## 4. 阵营规则

### 1. 狂暴破坏（WRECKA RAMPAGE）

**卡图**：[11-01-阵营规则-狂暴破坏.png](破坏专队-卡片提取/11-01-阵营规则-狂暴破坏.png)

**源页**：PDF 第 3 页

**中文全文**

爆炸声，刺鼻浓雾以及战斗中的尖叫驱使着破坏专
队做出更具破坏性的行为。
当一名己方破坏专队特工进行射击，近战或反击
时，在掷攻击骰步骤中：
• 每当您保留一枚结果为 6 的攻击骰，您便获得一
点破坏点数。
• 您可以花费至多 2 点您的破坏点数（炸弹跳跳除
外），每当您这样花费一点，便能将您的一枚失
败的掷骰结果作为一次普通成功保留，而不是将
其弃置。
您可以在同一次行动中获得和花费破坏点数，并且
可以按照自己选择的任何顺序进行，除非您的起始
点数为 6 点，在这种情况下，您只能选择花费它
们。

**English full text**

WRECKA KREW
FACTION RULE
WRECKA RAMPAGE
The sound of explosions, the acrid stench of smoke and
the screams of battle drive Wrecka Krews forwards to
ever more destructive acts.
Whenever a friendly WRECKA KREW operative
is shooting, fighting or retaliating, in the Roll
Attack Dice step:
• For each attack dice result of 6 you retain, you
gain one Wrecka point.
• You can spend up to 2 of your Wrecka points
(unless it’s a BOMB SQUIG, then you cannot
spend any). For each point you spend this way,
retain one of your fails as a normal success
instead of discarding it.
You cannot have more than 6 Wrecka points at
once. You can gain and spend Wrecka points
during the same action and can do so in an order
of your choice, unless you started the action with
6, in which case you can only spend them.

### 2. 泵入活力（TANKED UP）

**卡图**：[11-02-阵营规则-泵入活力.png](破坏专队-卡片提取/11-02-阵营规则-泵入活力.png)

**源页**：PDF 第 3 页

**中文全文**

劲爆战斗的快意赋予了破坏专队力量，为他们的莽
撞和残暴增添了新鲜活力。
在一名拥有交战命令的己方破坏专队特工（炸弹
跳跳除外）的每次转折点/反应期间，当其首次进行
冲锋、射击或近战行动（戒备除外）时，它的 APL
数值便在它的下一次激活开始前增加 1 点。

**English full text**

WRECKA KREW
FACTION RULE
TANKED UP
Wrecka Krews are empowered by the thrill of explosive
combat, lending them fresh impetus as they undertake
their murderous rampages.
The first time a friendly WRECKA KREW
operative (excluding BOMB SQUIG) that has an
Engage order performs either the Charge, Shoot
or Fight action (excluding Guard) during each of
its activations/counteractions, add 1 to its APL
stat until the start of its next activation.
