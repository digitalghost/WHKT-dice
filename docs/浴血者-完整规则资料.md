**中文全文**

怜悯是弱者才有的特质，如果将枪口对准自
己人能确保消灭敌人，浴血者并不会介意这
么做。
当一名己方浴血者特工执行射击行动，在选择
有效目标时使用此交战计谋。另一名己方浴血者
特工位于一名敌方特工控制范围内并不能阻止
那名敌方特工被选为有效目标。直到那个行动结
束前，每当您弃置一枚失败的攻击骰，您便需要

**English full text**

BLOODED
FIREFIGHT PLOY
CALLOUS DISREGARD
Pity is a trait of the weak. The Blooded will gladly turn
their guns upon their own kin if doing so will ensure the
destruction of an enemy.
Use this firefight ploy when a friendly BLOODED
operative performs the Shoot action and you’re
selecting a valid target. Having other friendly
BLOODED operatives within an enemy
operative’s control range doesn’t prevent that
enemy operative from being selected. Until
the end of that action, whenever you discard
an attack dice as a fail, inflict damage equal to
the dice result on one friendly operative of your
choice within control range of the target.

BLOODED
FIREFIGHT PLOY
MOMENT OF REPUTE
When infused with a portion of the Dark Gods’ power,

GAZE OF THE GODS, before or after it performs
an action. Until the end of that operative’s
activation, add 1 to its APL stat.

### 4. 黑暗青睐（DARK FAVOUR）

**卡图**：[30-04-交战计谋-黑暗青睐.png](浴血者-卡片提取/30-04-交战计谋-黑暗青睐.png)

**源页**：PDF 第 8 页

**中文全文**

那些在杀敌过程中流尽鲜血并获得那些炼狱守
护者青睐的人们或许能免遭死亡厄运——只要
有其他人代为付出代价即可。
当拥有一枚己方浴血指示物的一名己方浴血者
特工在一次近战行动中被选为近战目标或作为射
击行动的目标时使用此交战计谋。选择这位己方
特工 3" 内且可见的另一名己方浴血者特工成为
那次近战或射击行动的目标（视实际情况选择）
作为替代（忽略通常会造成无法选择的情况），
如果是近战行动，则在那次行动中将那位己方特
工视为位于近战特工的控制范围内。若那是一次
射击行动，那么其他特工只有在原本目标拥有掩
护或被遮挡时才能拥有对应的效果。
若那是一次射击行动且远程武器拥有“爆炸”
或“洪流”武器规则，此交战计谋无效。

**English full text**

BLOODED
FIREFIGHT PLOY
DARK FAVOUR
Those who have spilled the blood of the foe and earned
the favour of their infernal patron may be spared from
death, as long as another pays the price in their stead.
Use this firefight ploy when a friendly BLOODED
operative that has one of your Blooded tokens
is selected as the valid target of a Shoot action
or to fight against during the Fight action. Select
one other friendly BLOODED operative visible
to and within 3" of that first friendly operative to
become the valid target or to be fought against
(as appropriate) instead (even if it wouldn’t
normally be valid for this). If it’s the Fight action,
treat that other operative as being within the
fighting operative’s control range for the duration
of that action. If it’s the Shoot action, that other
operative is only in cover or obscured if the
original target was.
This ploy has no effect if it’s the Shoot action
and the ranged weapon has the Blast or Torrent
weapon rule.
