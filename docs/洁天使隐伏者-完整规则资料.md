### 2.1 小队概述

洁天使隐伏者杀戮小队由多种专家组成，每名成员均为追猎并消灭巫术敌人时的特定职责接受训练和配备装备。一些成员使用武装修会庞大武库中的神秘圣物武器与装备，携带这些圣物是一项重大的荣誉。

英文来源全文：

Celestian Insidiant kill teams comprise a range of specialists, each one trained and outfitted to perform a specific role
in the pursuit and destruction of sorcerous foes. Some utilise esoteric relic weapons and equipment drawn from the
extensive armouries of their Orders Militant, the bearing of which is a great honour.

### 2.2 阵容与配装约束

由 9 名特工组成：
- 1 名长执，选择“圣物降罪者掷桩器＋圣物爆矢手枪＋虚无之槌”，或“狱火手枪＋虚无之槌”。
- 8 名特工，从弃绝者、肃戒者、焰灭者、揭谕者、圣殁者、司圣者和战士中选择。
除焰灭者与战士外，每种专家最多选择一次；焰灭者最多两名，战士可重复。其他角色的武器为固定装备，多档案武器每次使用时按规则选择档案。

## 4. 阵营规则

### 祷祝（Benedictions）

ID：`IMP-CI-SUP-BEN`。术语／译名来源：PDF 第 4 页。消耗：无独立AP/CP消耗（具体条件见正文）。

接受祷祝的特工选择以下一个效果；残废的特工不能接受祷祝。
- 热忱（Ardour）：直到战斗结束，该特工的 APL 属性增加 1。不能为长执选择此祷祝。
- 怒火（Wrath）：直到战斗结束，该特工数据卡上的武器拥有“无休”（Ceaseless）武器规则。
- 复兴（Restoration）：该特工恢复至多 D3+2 点已损失的耐伤。
- 疾步（Exigence）：该特工可以立即免费执行一次冲锋或冲刺；若为冲锋，最多移动 3"。必须在距离那名已残废、处于圣勉状态的特工更近的位置结束移动。

英文当前全文：

|**Benediction**|**Effect**|
|-----|-----|
|Ardour|Until the end of the battle, add 1 to that operative’s APL stat. You cannot select this BENEDICTION for a SUPERIOR operative.|
|Wrath|Until the end of the battle, weapons on that operative’s datacard have the Ceaseless weapon rule.|
|Restoration|That operative regains up to D3+2 lost wounds.|
|Exigence|That operative can immediately perform a free Charge or Dash action (for the former, it cannot move more than 3"), but must end that move closer to that incapacitated INSPIRING operative.|


各角色对应外部ID：`IMP-CI-SUP-BEN`、`IMP-CI-ABJ-BEN`、`IMP-CI-CEN-BEN`、`IMP-CI-CRE-BEN`、`IMP-CI-DEN-BEN`、`IMP-CI-MOR-BEN`、`IMP-CI-REL-BEN`、`IMP-CI-WAR-BEN`

### 圣勉（Inspiration）

ID：`IMP-CI-SUP-INS`。术语／译名来源：PDF 第 5 页。消耗：无独立AP/CP消耗（具体条件见正文）。

每当一名己方洁天使隐伏者特工：
- 使一名耐伤属性为 6 或更高的敌方特工残废，该己方特工进入圣勉状态。
- 执行冲锋行动，在其移动前进入圣勉状态。
每当一名己方洁天使隐伏者特工处于圣勉状态，其数据卡上的武器拥有“严重”（Severe）武器规则。

英文当前全文：

Whenever a friendly CELESTIAN INSIDIANT operative:
- Incapacitates an enemy operative that has a Wounds stat of 6 or more, that friendly operative becomes INSPIRING.
- Performs the Charge action, before it moves, it becomes INSPIRING

Whenever a friendly CELESTIAN INSIDIANT operative is INSPIRING, weapons on its datacard have the Severe weapon rule.


各角色对应外部ID：`IMP-CI-SUP-INS`、`IMP-CI-ABJ-INS`、`IMP-CI-CEN-INS`、`IMP-CI-CRE-INS`、`IMP-CI-DEN-INS`、`IMP-CI-MOR-INS`、`IMP-CI-REL-INS`、`IMP-CI-WAR-INS`

### 殉道（Martyrdom）

ID：`IMP-CI-SUP-MAR`。术语／译名来源：PDF 第 4 页。消耗：无独立AP/CP消耗（具体条件见正文）。

每当一名处于圣勉状态的己方洁天使隐伏者特工残废，在将其移出杀戮区前，选择另一名己方洁天使隐伏者特工；残废特工必须对被选特工可见，且位于被选特工 6" 内。然后，被选特工获得一个祷祝。残废特工不能获得祷祝。

英文当前全文：

Whenever an INSPIRING friendly CELESTIAN INSIDIANT operative is incapacitated, before it’s removed from the killzone, select one other friendly CELESTIAN INSIDIANT operative that operative is visible to or within 6" of.

Then, that selected operative gains one BENEDICTION (incapacitated operatives cannot be selected for a BENEDICTION).

各角色对应外部ID：`IMP-CI-SUP-MAR`、`IMP-CI-ABJ-MAR`、`IMP-CI-CEN-MAR`、`IMP-CI-CRE-MAR`、`IMP-CI-DEN-MAR`、`IMP-CI-MOR-MAR`、`IMP-CI-REL-MAR`、`IMP-CI-WAR-MAR`

### 猎巫者的武备（Weapons Of The Witch Hunters）

ID：`IMP-CI-SUP-WOTWH`。术语／译名来源：PDF 第 4 页。消耗：无独立AP/CP消耗（具体条件见正文）。

灵能远程武器不能对己方洁天使隐伏者特工造成伤害。在灵能行动的效果中，己方洁天使隐伏者特工不能被选择，且永不视为位于这些行动要求的距离内。
每当一名特工位于一名己方洁天使隐伏者特工 3" 内：
- 其不能执行灵能行动，也不能使用灵能额外规则。
- 其不能使用灵能远程武器。
- 其灵能近战武器失去全部武器规则，且伤害属性不能高于 3/4。

英文当前全文：

PSYCHIC ranged weapons cannot inflict damage on friendly CELESTIAN INSIDIANT operatives.

For the effects of PSYCHIC actions, friendly CELESTIAN INSIDIANT operatives cannot be selected and are never treated as being within those actions’ required distances.
Whenever an operative is within 3" of a friendly CELESTIAN INSIDIANT operative:
- That operative cannot perform PSYCHIC actions or use PSYCHIC additional rules.
- That operative cannot use PSYCHIC ranged weapons.
- PSYCHIC melee weapons have no weapon rules and cannot have Dmg stats higher than 3/4.


各角色对应外部ID：`IMP-CI-SUP-WOTWH`、`IMP-CI-ABJ-WOTWH`、`IMP-CI-CEN-WOTWH`、`IMP-CI-CRE-WOTWH`、`IMP-CI-DEN-WOTWH`、`IMP-CI-MOR-WOTWH`、`IMP-CI-REL-WOTWH`、`IMP-CI-WAR-WOTWH`

本小队的一些武器拥有“反灵能者”武器规则：每当该武器用于拥有灵能者（PSYKER）关键词的特工时，获得“致命 5+”（Lethal 5+）武器规则。网站武器档案标有 Anti-PSYKER，但小队 API 未展开其定义；本定义按 PDF 第5页保留。

### 隐伏长执 / Superior

定位：领袖、圣勉支援。

#### 圣洁榜样（Holy Example）

ID：`IMP-CI-SUP-HE`。术语／译名来源：PDF 第 1 页。消耗：无独立AP/CP消耗（具体条件见正文）。

每个转折点一次，如果这名特工处于圣勉状态，可以使用一次 0CP 的交战计谋，但计谋指定的洁天使隐伏者特工必须是这名特工。这也包括为这名特工掷出的攻击骰或防御骰使用指挥重掷。

英文当前全文：

Once per turning point, if this operative is INSPIRING, you can use a firefight ploy for 0CP if this is the specified CELESTIAN INSIDIANT operative (including Command Re-roll if the attack or defence dice was rolled for this operative).

#### 灵魂导师（Spiritual Mentor）

ID：`IMP-CI-SUP-SM`。术语／译名来源：PDF 第 1 页。消耗：1 AP。

支援。选择对这名特工可见且位于其 6" 内的一名己方洁天使隐伏者特工，该特工进入圣勉状态。

英文当前全文：

**SUPPORT**. Select one friendly CELESTIAN INSIDIANT operative visible to and within 6" of this operative. That operative becomes INSPIRING.

This operative cannot perform this action while within control range of an enemy operative, or more than once per turning point.

### 隐伏弃绝者 / Abjuror

定位：护盾与护卫。

#### 护盾（*Shield）

ID：`IMP-CI-ABJ-SH`。术语／译名来源：PDF 第 1 页。消耗：无独立AP/CP消耗（具体条件见正文）。

每当这名特工使用此武器档案近战或反击，其每次格挡可以分配用于格挡两枚尚未结算的成功，而非一枚。

英文当前全文：

Whenever this operative is fighting or retaliating with this weapon profile, each of your blocks can be allocated to block two unresolved successes (instead of one).

#### 守圣者（Holy Defender）

ID：`IMP-CI-ABJ-HD`。术语／译名来源：PDF 第 1 页。消耗：无独立AP/CP消耗（具体条件见正文）。

每个转折点一次，当对这名特工可见且位于其 2" 内的另一名己方洁天使隐伏者特工被选为射击行动的有效目标或近战行动的近战对象时，可以使用。改由这名特工成为有效目标或近战对象，即使其通常不能如此被选。
若为近战行动，在该行动期间将这名特工视为位于执行近战的特工的控制范围内。若为射击行动，只有原目标拥有掩护或被遮挡时，这名特工才相应拥有掩护或被遮挡。
若为射击行动且远程武器拥有“爆炸”或“洪流”规则，此规则无效。

英文当前全文：

Once per turning point, when a friendly CELESTIAN INSIDIANT operative visible to and within 2" of this operative is selected as the valid target of a Shoot action or to fight against during the Fight action, you can use this rule.
If you do, this operative becomes the valid target or is fought against (as appropriate) instead (even if it wouldn’t normally be valid for this). If it’s the Fight action, treat this operative as being within the fighting operative’s control range for the duration of that action.
If it’s the Shoot action, this operative is only in cover or obscured if the original target was.

This rule has no effect if it’s the Shoot action and the ranged weapon has the Blast or Torrent weapon rule.

### 隐伏肃戒者 / Censor

英文当前全文：

This operative starts the battle with a null range of 1".
Whenever an enemy operative is within null range of this operative, subtract 2" from the Move stat of that enemy operative and worsen the Hit stat of its weapons by 1 (this isn’t cumulative with being injured).

#### 虚无仪式（Nullifying Ritual）

ID：`IMP-CI-CEN-NR`。术语／译名来源：PDF 第 2 页。消耗：1 AP。

这名特工的虚无范围增加 1"，最大为 5"。标识／指示物指南中的虚无指示物带有数字，使用数字等于其虚无范围的指示物。
这名特工位于敌方特工的控制范围内时不能执行此行动；每个转折点最多执行一次。

英文当前全文：

Add 1 to this operative’s null range (to a maximum of 5"). Nullification tokens in the marker/token guide are numbered, so use the numbered token equal to this operative’s null range.

This operative cannot perform this action while within control range of an enemy operative, or more than once per turning point.

#### 手执告诫之杖（Virge of Admonition Icon Bearer）

ID：`IMP-CI-CEN-VOAIB`。术语／译名来源：PDF 第 2 页。消耗：无独立AP/CP消耗（具体条件见正文）。

英文当前全文：

Whenever determining control of a marker, treat this operative’s APL stat as 1 higher. Note this isn't a change to its APL stat, so any changes are cumulative with this.

Once per turning point, when this operative inflicts damage on an enemy operative with either profile of its hand flamer but doesn’t incapacitate it, you can use this rule.
If you do, you can select one friendly CELESTIAN INSIDIANT operative within 6" of this operative to become INSPIRING.

Whenever this operative is INSPIRING, the Suspect & Eliminate strategy ploy costs you 0CP if the enemy operative or marker you select is visible to or within 6" of this operative.

支援。选择对这名特工可见、位于其 6" 内且处于圣勉状态的一名己方洁天使隐伏者特工；被选特工不再处于圣勉状态。然后选择对这名特工可见且位于其 6" 内的另一名己方洁天使隐伏者特工，为其结算殉道阵营规则中的一个祷祝，但不能选择疾步。

**SUPPORT.** Select an INSPIRING friendly CELESTIAN INSIDIANT operative visible to and within 6" of this operative.

#### 炽誓绝谕（Zealous Ultimatum）

ID：`IMP-CI-MOR-ZU`。术语／译名来源：PDF 第 3 页。消耗：无独立AP/CP消耗（具体条件见正文）。

每场战斗一次，战略计谋。选择位于这名特工 8" 内的一名敌方特工并向其发出绝谕。对手必须接受或拒绝；如果目标是非玩家特工，掷一枚 D6，4+ 为接受，否则为拒绝。
若接受，每当这名特工与该敌方特工近战或反击，其受祝阔剑的攻击属性增加 1。每场战斗中，这名特工首次在近战或反击时使该敌方特工残废后，其受祝阔剑的攻击属性增加 1，持续至战斗结束。两种情况下攻击属性最多为 5。
若拒绝，每当该敌方特工与一名己方洁天使隐伏者特工近战或反击，其近战武器的攻击属性减少 1。

英文当前全文：

Once per battle STRATEGIC GAMBIT. Select one enemy operative within 8" of this operative and issue an ultimatum to it.
Your opponent must accept or decline that ultimatum (if it's a non-player operative, roll one D6: on a 4+, the ultimatum is accepted, otherwise it’s declined).

If the ultimatum is accepted, whenever this operative is fighting against or retaliating against that enemy operative, add 1 to the Atk stat of this operative's blessed broadsword.
The first time this operative incapacitates that enemy operative while fighting or retaliating during the battle, add 1 to the Atk stat of this operative’s blessed broadsword until the end of the battle.
In either case, this is to a maximum of 5.

If the ultimatum is declined, whenever that enemy operative is fighting against or retaliating against a friendly CELESTIAN INSIDIANT operative, subtract 1 from the Atk stat of that enemy operative’s weapons.

英文当前全文：

At the end of each of this operative’s activations, if it’s INSPIRING and it controls an objective marker or one of your mission markers, one friendly CELESTIAN INSIDIANT operative this operative is visible to and within 6" of becomes INSPIRING.

#### 虚无圣像持有者（Simulacrum Nullificatus Icon Bearer）

Note this isn’t a change to the APL stat, so any changes are cumulative with this.

轻型路障属于轻型地形，但支脚属于无关紧要和暴露地形。战斗开始前，可以部署任意数量的所选路障，必须完全位于己方领地、位于杀戮区地面，并与其他装备地形、入口及可进入地形相距超过 2"。

英文当前全文：

Light barricades are Light terrain, except the feet, which are Insignificant and Exposed. Before the battle, you can set up any of them wholly within your territory, on the killzone floor and more than 2" from other equipment terrain features, access points, and Accessible terrain.

### 重型路障（1个）（Heavy Barricade (x1)）

ID：`UNIVERSAL-UE-HB`；网站独有，无本队卡图。

重型路障属于重型地形。战斗开始前，可以将其放置在完全位于己方降落区 4" 内、杀戮区地面上，且与其他装备地形、入口及可进入地形相距超过 2" 的位置。

英文当前全文：

A heavy barricade is Heavy terrain. Before the battle, you can set it up wholly within 4" of your drop zone, on the killzone floor and more than 2" from other equipment terrain features, access points, and Accessible terrain.

### 梯子（2个）（Ladders (x2)）

ID：`UNIVERSAL-UE-LAD`；网站独有，无本队卡图。

梯子属于无关紧要和暴露地形。战斗开始前可以部署任意数量的所选梯子，须满足：
- 完全位于己方领地。
- 直立靠在至少 2" 高的地形上。
- 与其他装备地形相距超过 2"。
- 与门及入口相距超过 1"。
每次行动一次，特工攀爬梯子所靠的地形时，如果整个攀爬过程中梯子都位于其控制范围内，将该次垂直距离视为 1"。

英文当前全文：

Ladders are Insignificant and Exposed terrain. Before the battle, you can set up any of them as follows:
- Wholly within your territory.
- Upright against terrain that’s at least 2" tall.
- More than 2" from other equipment terrain features.
- More than 1" from doors and access points.

Once per action, whenever an operative is climbing the terrain feature a ladder is placed against, treat the vertical distance as 1" if the ladder is within that operative’s control range during that entire climb.

### 便携式路障（1个）（Portable Barricade (x1)）

ID：`UNIVERSAL-UE-PB`；网站独有，无本队卡图。

便携式路障属于轻型、防护和便携地形，但支脚属于无关紧要和暴露地形。战斗开始前，可以将其放在完全位于己方领地、杀戮区地面上，且与其他装备地形、入口及可进入地形相距超过 2" 的位置。
防护：特工从此地形获得掩护时，其豁免属性改善1，最好为2+。
便携：仅当特工与其接触且盾面造成阻碍时提供掩护，忽略支脚。与内侧接触的特工可以执行以下独特行动。
携障移动（1AP）：如同转移行动，但行动特工最多移动其移动属性减去2"的距离，不能攀爬、跳落、跳跃，也不能使用任何将特工移除并重新放置的小队规则，例如炉裔打捞者的飞行或曼德拉的暗影通道。移动前移除与其接触的便携式路障；移动后重新放置，使其再次与特工接触，但不能放在其他装备地形、入口或可进入地形的2"内。
此行动视为转移行动。位于敌方特工控制范围内时不能执行；也不能在已执行后撤或冲锋的同一次激活中执行。

英文当前全文：

A portable barricade is Light, Protective and Portable terrain, except the feet which are Insignificant and Exposed.
 Before the battle, you can set it up wholly within your territory, on the killzone floor and more than 2" from other equipment terrain features, access points, and Accessible terrain.
   
 
 **Protective:** While an operative is in cover from this terrain feature, improve its Save stat by 1 (to a maximum of 2+).  
 
 **Portable:** This terrain feature only provides cover while an operative is connected to it and if the shield is intervening (ignore its feet).
 Operatives connected to the inside of it can perform the following unique action during the battle:  
 
 **Move With Barricade (1 AP):**
 The same as the Reposition action, except the active operative can move no more than its Move stat minus 2" and cannot climb, drop, jump, or use any killteam's rules that remove it and set it back up again (e.gh. HEARTHKYN SALVAGER Fly, MANDRAKE Shadow Passage).  
 
 Before this operative moves, remove the portable barricade it is connected to. After it moves, set up the portable barricade so it is connected again, but the portable barricade cannot be set up within 2" of
 other equipment terrain features, access points or Accessible terrain. If this is not possible, the portable barricade is not set up again.  
 
 This action is treated as a Reposition action. An operative cannot perform this action while within control range of an enemy operative, or in the same activation in which it performed the Fall Back or Charge action.

### 功能型手雷—烟雾（Utility Grenade - Smoke (x1/2)）

ID：`UNIVERSAL-UE-UG-SMK`；网站独有，无本队卡图。

选择此装备时，选择两枚功能型手雷：两枚烟雾、两枚震荡，或各一枚。每个选项赋予己方特工可执行的独特行动，但全队每场战斗执行该行动的总次数不得超过所选该类手雷的数量。
烟雾手雷（1AP）：
- 将一枚己方烟雾手雷标识放置在这名特工6"内，且必须对其可见；也可以放在对其可见的地形模型的制高点地形上。标识在其水平方向1"内、垂直上方无限高处形成烟雾区域，但不延伸到下方。
- 完全位于烟雾中的特工对于距其超过2"的特工被遮挡，反之亦然。此外，射击完全位于烟雾中的敌方特工时，拥有穿刺2或关键穿刺2的武器分别改为穿刺1或关键穿刺1，除非双方在彼此2"内。
- 下一次战略阶段的备战步骤，掷一枚D3；当等于结果次数的激活完成后，或在该转折点结束时（取最先者），移除烟雾标识。
- 位于敌方特工控制范围内，或全队已达到使用次数上限时，不能执行此行动。

英文当前全文：

When you select this equipment, select two utility grenades (2 smoke, 2 stun, or 1 smoke and 1 stun).
 Each selection is a unique action your operatives can perform, but your kill team can only perform that action a total number of times during the battle equal to your selection.
   
 
 **SMOKE GRENADE (1 AP):**  
 
 
 - Place one of your Smoke Grenade markers within 6" of this operative. It must be visible to this operative,
 or on Vantage terrain of a terrain feature that is visible to this operative. The marker creates an area of smoke 1" horizontally and unlimited height vertically from (but not below) it.
 - While an operative is wholly within an area of smoke, it is obscured to operatives more than 2" from it, and vice versa.
 In addition, whenever an operative is shooting an enemy operative wholly within an area of smoke, weapons with the Piercing 2 or Piercing Crits 2 weapon rule have the Piercing 1 or Piercing Crits 1 weapon rule (respectively) instead, unless they’re within 2" of each other.
 - In the Ready step of the next Strategy phase, roll one D3. Remove that Smoke Grenade marker after a number of activations equal to that D3 have been completed
 or at the end of the turning point (whichever comes first).
 - An operative cannot perform this action while within control range of an enemy operative, or if you have reached the total number of times your kill team can perform it.

### 破障炸药（Breaching Charge）

ID：`UNIVERSAL-UE-BC`；网站独有，无本队卡图。

每场战斗一次，一名己方特工执行破障（Breach）行动时可以使用。该行动的AP消耗减少1，最低为1AP，并视为该特工数据卡上具有“破障标识”（breach marker）字样。

英文当前全文：

Once per battle, when a friendly operative performs the Breach action, you can use this rule. If you do, that operative can perform that action for 1 less AP (to a minimum of 1AP) as though it had the word "breach marker" on its datacard.
