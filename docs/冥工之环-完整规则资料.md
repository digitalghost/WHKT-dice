### 2.1 小队概述

冥工之环由受冥工技师掌控的异形构体组成。这些无情而非生物的冥工构体扫除主人野心道路上的障碍，无论岩石、金属还是活物。技师通过能量与数据节点进一步强化这些古老构体的能力。

英文来源全文：

Canoptek Circle kill teams comprise sinister alien constructs in thrall to their Cryptek master. The emotionless and unliving Canoptek constructs overcome obstacles to a Cryptek’s ambitions, whether rock and metal or the bodies of the living, while their master accesses nodes of power and data to charge the constructs’ ancient abilities further.

## 4. 阵营规则

### 方尖碑节点矩阵（Obelisk Node Matrix）

ID：`NEC-CAN-GEO-ONM`。术语／译名来源：PDF 第 4、4 页。消耗：无独立AP/CP消耗（具体条件见正文）。

作为第一个转折点的战略计谋，将己方三枚方尖碑节点标识放置在完全位于己方领地的位置。作为第一个转折点之后各转折点的战略计谋，可以将每枚己方方尖碑节点标识水平移动至多 3"。
己方方尖碑节点标识可以控制其 1" 内、没有敌方特工争夺的其他标识（为此将方尖碑节点标识视为己方特工）。如果多名玩家的方尖碑节点标识会控制同一个标识，则没有方尖碑节点标识控制它。
每当一枚己方方尖碑节点标识在水平方向上位于另一枚己方方尖碑节点标识 6" 内，这些标识及其间的区域形成方尖碑节点矩阵，并延伸至上方与下方（杀戮区中的高度不影响此效果）。如果三枚标识都满足条件，则形成更大的组合矩阵。
每当一名己方冥工之环特工位于己方方尖碑节点矩阵内：
- 其数据卡上的武器拥有“精准 1”（Accurate 1）武器规则。
- 其 APL 属性增加 1，最多为 3。

英文当前全文：

As a STRATEGIC GAMBIT in the first turning point, place your three OBELISK NODE markers wholly within your territory. As a STRATEGIC GAMBIT in each turning point after the first, you can move each of your OBELISK NODE markers up to 3" horizontally.
 
Your OBELISK NODE markers control other markers within 1" of them that no enemy operatives contest (treat your OBELISK NODE markers as friendly operatives for this purpose).
If more than one player would use their OBELISK NODE markers to control the same marker, no OBELISK NODE markers control it.

Whenever one of your OBELISK NODE markers is within 6" horizontally of another of your OBELISK NODE markers, those markers and the area between them create an OBELISK NODE MATRIX above and below (in other words, their height in the killzone is irrelevant).
If all three of your OBELISK NODE markers fulfil this, it creates a larger combined OBELISK NODE MATRIX.
Whenever a friendly CANOPTEK CIRCLE operative is within your OBELISK NODE MATRIX:
- Weapons on its datacard have the Accurate 1 weapon rule.
- Add 1 to its APL stat (to a maximum of 3).

### 地相技师 / Geomancer

定位：领袖、节点控制与支援。

#### 控制冥工（Canoptek Control）

ID：`NEC-CAN-GEO-CC`。术语／译名来源：PDF 第 1 页。消耗：1 AP。

支援。选择这名特工可见且位于其 6" 内的一名己方冥工之环冥工（CANOPTEK）特工。也可以改为选择这名特工可见且位于己方方尖碑节点矩阵内的一名己方冥工之环冥工特工（此种选择不适用“支援”）。被选特工可以立即免费执行一次消耗 1AP 的行动；在该行动中不能移动超过 2"，若被移除并重新放置，则必须完全放置在原位置 2" 内。
这名特工位于敌方特工的控制范围内或正在反应时，不能执行此行动。

英文当前全文：

SUPPORT. Select one friendly CANOPTEK CIRCLE CANOPTEK operative visible to and within 6" of this operative. Alternatively, you can select one friendly CANOPTEK CIRCLE CANOPTEK operative that’s visible to this operative and within your OBELISK NODE MATRIX (SUPPORT doesn’t apply to this selection).
 That selected operative can immediately perform a 1AP action for free; during that action, it cannot move more than 2", or must be set up wholly within 2" if it’s removed and set up again.
 
 This operative cannot perform this action while within control range of an enemy operative, or while counteracting.

#### 地相干扰（Geomantic Disturbance）

ID：`NEC-CAN-GEO-GD`。术语／译名来源：PDF 第 1 页。消耗：1 AP。

选择一个地形模型上的点，该点必须位于这名特工 8" 内且对其可见。为该点 2" 内的每名特工分别掷 2D6；如果结果高于该特工的剩余耐伤，则对其造成等于两者差值的伤害。
这名特工拥有隐匿命令或位于敌方特工的控制范围内时，不能执行此行动。

英文当前全文：

Select a point on a terrain feature; that point must be visible to and within 8" of this operative. Separately roll 2D6 for each operative within 2" of that point. If the result is higher than that operative’s remaining wounds, inflict damage on it equal to the difference.

This operative cannot perform this action while it has a Conceal order, or while within control range of an enemy operative.

#### 分子突破（Molecular Breach）

ID：`NEC-CAN-GEO-MB`。术语／译名来源：PDF 第 1 页。消耗：1 AP。

支援。选择这名特工可见且位于其 6" 内的一名己方冥工之环特工。也可以改为选择这名特工可见且位于己方方尖碑节点矩阵内的一名己方冥工之环特工（此种选择不适用“支援”）。
被选特工下次执行包含移动的行动时，不移动模型，而是将其移出杀戮区，然后重新放置在完全位于原位置水平距离不超过其移动属性的位置；若为冲刺，则该距离为 3"。在使用近距离战斗规则的杀戮区（例如墓穴世界），可以跨越或穿过墙壁地形测量，并可放置在另一侧。冲锋不会因此获得额外距离。
必须放在模型能够合法放置的位置；除非执行的是冲锋，否则不能放置在敌方特工的控制范围内。
这名特工位于敌方特工的控制范围内时，不能执行此行动。

英文当前全文：

SUPPORT. Select one friendly CANOPTEK CIRCLE operative visible to and within 6" of this operative. Alternatively, you can select one friendly CANOPTEK CIRCLE operative visible to this operative and within your OBELISK NODE MATRIX (SUPPORT doesn’t apply to this selection).

The next time the selected operative performs an action in which it moves, don’t move it. Instead, remove it from the killzone and set it back up wholly within a distance equal to its Move stat (or 3" if it was a Dash) horizontally of its
