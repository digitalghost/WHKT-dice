**中文全文**

远猎者族团依靠着本能进行移动和战斗，以

势。
在每个战略阶段的就绪步骤中，您可以为最多三
名不位于敌方特工控制范围内的己方远猎者族团
特工更改命令。
每当您进行反应时，作为替代您可以更改一名不
位于敌方特工控制范围内的己方远猎者族团特
工的命令。这仍算作您进行了反应（之后换作您
的对手激活），但不算作那名己方特工在这个转
折点中的反应。

**English full text**

FARSTALKER KINBAND
FACTION RULE
FARSTALKER
Farstalker Kindbands move and fight instinctively,
reacting to developing battlefield conditions with an
inhuman speed of thought.
In the Ready step of each Strategy phase, you
can change the order of up to three friendly
FARSTALKER KINBAND operatives that are not
within control range of enemy operatives.
Whenever it’s your turn to counteract, you can
change the order of one friendly FARSTALKER
KINBAND operative that’s not within control
range of enemy operatives instead. This still
counts as you counteracting (so activation
alternates back to your opponent afterwards),
