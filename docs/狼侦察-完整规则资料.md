### 2.1 小队概述

狼侦察杀戮小队是所属大连中最令人畏惧、最有成就的猎手。他们远远行进在太空野狼突击部队前方，独自执行没有支援的任务，通过刺杀、破坏等手段在敌后制造死亡与混乱，并常以奇异迷雾或猛烈风暴遮蔽行踪。

英文来源全文：

Wolf Scout kill teams are their Great Company's most feared and accomplished hunters, roving far ahead of the Space Wolves' strike forces on solitary, unsupported missions. They cause death and anarchy behind enemy lines through assassination, sabotage and more besides, often while cloaked in strange mists or furious storms.

### 2.2 阵容与配装约束

由 6 名特工组成：1 名芬里斯狼，以及从狼群领袖、獠牙使者、霜眼、炮手、陷阱大师、符文祭司诗人、猎手中选择的 5 名狼侦察特工。除猎手可重复外，列表中的每种特工最多选择一次。网站构成没有另行强制选择狼群领袖。
角色武器均为其固定装备，等离子武器的标准／过载是同一件武器的可选档案。

## 4. 阵营规则

### 元素风暴（Elemental Storm）

ID：`IMP-WS-LDR-ES`。术语／译名来源：PDF 第 4 页。消耗：无独立AP/CP消耗（具体条件见正文）。

战略计谋。将己方风暴标识移出杀戮区（若有），然后放置在杀戮区中。每当一名特工完全位于该标识水平距离 6" 内，其完全位于己方风暴内。
每名己方狼侦察特工可以在拥有隐匿命令时执行冲锋行动，但必须在己方风暴内结束该行动。

英文当前全文：

STRATEGIC GAMBIT. Remove your Storm marker from the killzone (if any), then place it in the killzone.
 Whenever an operative is wholly within 6" horizontally of your Storm marker, it’s wholly within your STORM.
 
 Each friendly WOLF SCOUT operative can perform the Charge action while it has a Conceal order if it ends that action within your STORM.

各角色对应外部ID：`IMP-WS-LDR-ES`、`IMP-WS-FB-ES`、`IMP-WS-FE-ES`、`IMP-WS-GNR-ES`、`IMP-WS-HNT-ES`、`IMP-WS-RP-ES`、`IMP-WS-TM-ES`、`IMP-WS-WLF-ES`

### 猎手阿斯塔特（Hunting Astartes）

ID：`IMP-WS-LDR-HA`。术语／译名来源：PDF 第 4 页。消耗：无独立AP/CP消耗（具体条件见正文）。

在每名己方狼侦察特工的激活期间，其可以执行两次射击行动或两次近战行动。若执行两次射击：
- 如果两次射击都使用等离子枪或等离子手枪，第二次行动必须额外消耗 1AP。
- 不能选择两件灵能远程武器。
每名己方狼侦察特工均可反应，无论其命令为何。若其在完全位于己方风暴内时反应，可以先更改命令，或以更改命令代替执行行动；后一种情况下，仍视为其已在本转折点反应。

英文当前全文：

During each friendly WOLF SCOUT operative’s activation, it can perform either two Shoot actions or two Fight actions. If it’s two Shoot actions:
- 1 additional AP must be spent for the second action if both actions are using a plasma gun or plasma pistol.
- You cannot select two PSYCHIC ranged weapons.

Each friendly WOLF SCOUT operative can counteract regardless of its order. Whenever it does so wholly within your STORM, you can change its order first, or change its order instead of performing an action (for the latter, still treat it as having counteracted this turning point).

各角色对应外部ID：`IMP-WS-LDR-HA`、`IMP-WS-FB-HA`、`IMP-WS-FE-HA`、`IMP-WS-GNR-HA`、`IMP-WS-HNT-HA`、`IMP-WS-RP-HA`、`IMP-WS-TM-HA`、`IMP-WS-WLF-HA`

## 6. 角色定位与专属能力

### 狼侦察狼群领袖 / Pack Leader

定位：领袖、先攻干预。

#### 沧桑老兵（Grizzled Veteran）

ID：`IMP-WS-LDR-GV`。术语／译名来源：PDF 第 1 页。消耗：无独立AP/CP消耗（具体条件见正文）。

这名特工在本场战斗中首次将要残废时，不会残废，而是剩余 1 点耐伤，且在该行动余下期间不能残废。丢弃所有剩余攻击骰，包括这名特工近战或反击时的己方骰子。该行动结束时，不能使用“反击”交战计谋，也不能通过“狂野斗士”战略计谋造成伤害。

英文当前全文：

The first time this operative would be incapacitated during the battle, it is not incapacitated, has 1 wound remaining and cannot be incapacitated for the remainder of the action. All remaining attack dice are discarded (including yours if this operative is fighting or retaliating). You cannot use the Counterattack firefight ploy or inflict damage as a result of the Savage Fighters strategy ploy at the end of that action.

#### 狡诈如狼（Lupine Guile）

ID：`IMP-WS-LDR-LG`。术语／译名来源：PDF 第 1 页。消耗：无独立AP/CP消耗（具体条件见正文）。

每场战斗一次，在掷骰决定先手之后，如果这名特工位于杀戮区中，可以重掷己方的骰子。

英文当前全文：

Once per battle, after rolling off to decide initiative, if this operative is in the killzone, you can re-roll your dice.

### 狼侦察獠牙使者 / Fangbearer

定位：医疗与伤势支援。

#### 治疗膏药（Healing Balms）

ID：`IMP-WS-FB-HB`。术语／译名来源：PDF 第 1 页。消耗：1 AP。

选择位于这名特工控制范围内的一名己方狼侦察特工，其恢复至多 D3+3 点已损失的耐伤。
这名特工位于敌方特工的控制范围内时不能执行此行动；每个转折点最多执行一次。

英文当前全文：

Select one friendly WOLF SCOUT operative within this operative’s control range to regain up to D3+3 lost wounds.
 
 This operative cannot perform this action while within control range of an enemy operative, or more than once per turning point.

Each selection is a unique action your operatives can perform, but your kill team can only perform that action a total number of times during the battle equal to your selection.
  

**STUN GRENADE (1 AP):**  


- Select one enemy operative visible to and within 6" of this operative. That operative and each other operative within 1" of it takes a stun test. For an operative to take a stun test, roll one D6: on a 3+, subtract 1 from its APL stat until the end of its next activation.
- An operative cannot perform this action while within control range of an enemy operative, or if you have reached the total number of times your kill team can perform it.

### 破障炸药（Breaching Charge）

ID：`UNIVERSAL-UE-BC`；网站独有，无本队卡图。

每场战斗一次，一名己方特工执行破障（Breach）行动时可以使用。该行动的AP消耗减少1，最低为1AP，并视为该特工数据卡上具有“破障标识”（breach marker）字样。

英文当前全文：

Once per battle, when a friendly operative performs the Breach action, you can use this rule. If you do, that operative can perform that action for 1 less AP (to a minimum of 1AP) as though it had the word "breach marker" on its datacard.
